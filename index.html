About Semadyme Classic Trade Finance Co

Semadyme Classic Trade Finance Co (also operating as Semadyme Classic Corporate Banking Limited) is a premier financial services institution strategically located in the heart of Port Harcourt's commercial district. We specialize in trade finance, corporate banking, and investment advisory services designed specifically for Nigerian businesses.

With rigorous risk management protocols led by experienced financial professionals, we ensure every transaction meets the highest standards of security and compliance. Our location at 120 Uniport Road, Obio Akpor, positions us to serve businesses across Rivers State and beyond.

Our mission is to bridge the financing gap for small, medium, and large enterprises through accessible, transparent, and professionally managed financial products. We understand the unique challenges of the Nigerian business landscape and provide customized solutions that align with your strategic objectives.

Comprehensive Financial Solutions

Trade Finance

We facilitate international and domestic trade transactions with letters of credit, trade credit insurance, invoice financing, and supply chain finance solutions. Our trade finance products ensure smooth cash flow for importers and exporters across various sectors.

Corporate Banking

Tailored banking solutions for businesses including business accounts, cash management services, corporate loans, overdraft facilities, and working capital financing. We support your operational efficiency with dedicated relationship managers.

Investment Advisory

Strategic investment guidance for portfolio diversification, treasury management, and wealth preservation. Our expert advisors help you make informed decisions aligned with your long-term financial goals.

Risk Management

Comprehensive risk assessment and mitigation strategies led by certified risk officers. We protect your business interests through thorough due diligence, credit analysis, and ongoing risk monitoring.

Foreign Exchange Services

Competitive forex rates and currency hedging solutions for businesses engaged in international trade. We provide timely execution and transparent pricing for all currency transactions.

Asset Finance

Equipment leasing, asset-backed lending, and project finance solutions that enable business expansion without depleting working capital. Flexible repayment terms designed for Nigerian business cycles.
