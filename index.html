About Us

Edu'uess Enterprise is a leading manufacturing company based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State. Located at 120 Uniport Road, opposite Lapo Macrofinance Bank in Choba, we are strategically positioned to serve clients across Obio Akpor and the entire South-South region.

With our commitment to excellence and quality, we provide reliable manufacturing solutions that meet international standards while understanding the unique needs of Nigerian businesses. Our state-of-the-art facility and experienced team ensure every project is executed with precision and professionalism.
